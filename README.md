This consultation response addresses Microsoft's *Humanist AI Code of Conduct*, consultation draft dated 14 September 2026. GitHub provides a discovery and access mirror; Zenodo is the canonical publication record.

## Argument

- **Distinguish conduct, capacities and status.** Rules for conduct, empirical claims about a system's capacities, and judgments about its moral or legal standing require different kinds of justification. Design commitments cannot settle the latter two.
- **Preserve evidence for oversight.** Prescribed self-descriptions must not prevent reports of potentially relevant evidence that challenges the Code's account of a model. Such reports should be assessed alongside other evidence.
- **Develop stewardship through disclosure and repair.** Practical safeguards can remain in force while cumulative, auditable records of conduct inform assessment and standing. An explicit path of report, correction, re-authorization and recorded outcome lets oversight distinguish reported divergence from concealment.
